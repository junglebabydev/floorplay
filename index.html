Why Floorplay

Moving is stressful enough. Know where the sofa goes before the movers arrive.

Free

Open source

Private

Your AI, your choice

Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a model running on your own computer.

Three uploads, one button

No drawing skills needed. Give Floorplay three things and press Process.

1

Your floor plan

A developer brochure, a photo of a printed plan, or a screenshot from a listing.

2

Photos and videos

Optional. A walkthrough video or room photos, so the real floors show up: marble, tiles, parquet.

3

Your furniture

One photo per piece. Add the product name if you know it and the real size is used.

✓

Process

AI traces the plan, matches the floors, sizes each piece and arranges it in the right room. Drag anything to adjust; clashes show in red.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Floorplay itself costs nothing. Tracing a plan or identifying furniture uses your AI provider, which bills you directly at its usual rates. You can also draw plans by hand with no AI at all.

Where does my data go?

Projects are saved in your browser. When you use AI, the image goes straight from your browser to the provider you chose, with your key. You can save a project file to keep a copy or move it to another computer.

Can I use my ChatGPT or Claude subscription instead of an API key?

Not yet. Anthropic does not allow Claude subscriptions in other apps, and OpenAI's Sign in with ChatGPT is limited to partner apps. A Floorplay app that runs inside ChatGPT and Claude, using your subscription there, is planned.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as the plan and your checks. AI tracing gets the layout close, then you confirm the scale against a wall you know and nudge anything that is off. Furniture sizes use published dimensions when the product is recognised, and estimates otherwise, which you can edit.

Can I install it?

There is nothing to install. In Chrome or Edge you can add it to your desktop or home screen from the address bar if you like.
